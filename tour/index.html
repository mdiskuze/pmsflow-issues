Cycle tracker for Android

A cycle tracker that keeps your data on your phone

PMS Flow keeps track of your periods, symptoms and estimates. Everything stays on your phone, there is no account and no cloud.

  • No account, no cloud
  • No advertising, no analytics
  • Backups you can protect with your own password
  • In English and Czech

PMS Flow is not a medical device. Estimates are estimates, and they are not a method of contraception. Android 8.0 or newer.

Calendar

A calendar that shows where you are in your cycle

The calendar opens with the cycle ring: the day of the running cycle, the phase you are in and the estimate of the next period.

  • Colours tell the days apart: period, predicted period (a dashed outline), fertile window, ovulation, symptoms and pregnancy.
  • Days of the same kind form one band, so a period or a fertile window reads at a glance.
  • The legend under the month switches kinds on and off. The filter button next to Today opens the same switches in one list.
  • Tap a day for its details, long press it for quick actions.

Logging

Log a period, a symptom or a pregnancy in a few taps

The plus button offers the usual things. A long press does the fastest one for today.

  • Log the start of a period, with an optional end date. Leave the end empty while the period is going on.
  • Set the flow strength for the whole period, and change it for a single day when you need to.
  • Log symptoms: cramps, mood and energy, mid-cycle pain, cervical mucus, basal body temperature and notes.
  • Log a pregnancy. It closes by itself when you log the first period after it.
  • Edit or delete from the day details, a single day or the whole record.

Estimates

Estimates that say how sure they are

PMS Flow estimates the next period, ovulation and the fertile window from your own history, and tells you how much to trust them.

  • Confidence is shown as Low, Medium or High, based on how regular your past cycles were.
  • The standard fertile window has 6 days: five days before ovulation and the day of ovulation. The conservative mode shows a wider window when the timing is uncertain.
  • Gradual fertility only changes the strength of the colour, never which days are marked.
  • Irregular cycle mode is for variable cycles (stress, postpartum, perimenopause, PCOS) and uses a wider tolerance.
  • Show prediction range displays a window of dates instead of one exact day.

Statistics

Your history in numbers and charts

Statistics show your whole history, and you can narrow it to the last 3, 6 or 12 months.

  • An overview: the average cycle length, the average period length and the number of cycles and periods.
  • The most common flow strength, cramp level and mood.
  • Insights in plain words, for example when cramps tend to start before a period.
  • Charts of the cycle length over time, the period duration, the distribution of the flow strength, and the trends of cramps, mood and energy.

Sharing

Share with a partner or family, without any cloud

You can hand someone a read-only snapshot as a .pmsshare file. They see it, and they cannot change it.

  • You choose what goes in: an alias, and whether symptoms and notes are included.
  • A file you receive opens as a separate read-only profile and is never mixed with your own records.
  • You can rename a profile, give it a relationship label, sort and filter the list and keep earlier versions from the same sender.

Backup

Backups you control

You can save your records to a file, send them on, or let the app back them up every day.

  • Choose CSV (two files: periods and symptoms) or JSON (one file, the complete backup), and a range of dates.
  • Save to a file writes the backup where you choose. Share sends the file itself to another app.
  • A backup password protects every new JSON backup, also the automatic one. Keep it safe, a protected backup cannot be opened without it. Without a password the app warns you in plain words.
  • The automatic backup saves a complete backup once a day into a folder you pick, or into the PMSFlow subfolder of the folder for downloaded files, and keeps the last 7 days.
  • Restoring asks whether to keep your records and add the new ones, or to replace the records on the same dates. Before a restore a copy of your current records is saved into the backup folder, and when that copy cannot be saved, the restore does not start.

Settings

Settings, group by group

The gear at the top right opens the settings.

  • General: the language (English or Czech), the theme (like the system, light or dark) and colours taken from your wallpaper on Android 12 and newer. The colours of the calendar stay the same.
  • Security: an app lock with biometric unlock or a PIN, asked for when you come back to the app.
  • Cycle predictions: the range of months to look ahead, gradual fertility, the fertility window mode, the prediction range and the irregular cycle mode.
  • Notifications: a period reminder (how many days before), a late period check, a symptom reminder at a time you choose and a fertile days reminder.
  • My data: backup and restore, clearing all data, an optional import of the basal body temperature from Health Connect (read only), and a page about how your data is handled.

Help

Short answers inside the app

The How-to tab has short, practical tips for common situations, and the info button at the top right shows the version.

  • How to log a period, an ongoing period, the irregular cycle mode and how to read the fertile window.
  • The info dialog shows the version and the recent changes. The version from GitHub can also check for updates there.

Privacy

What happens to your data

Short and plain. The full text is in the privacy policy.

  • All your records are stored on your phone. There is no account and the developer has no server that receives them.
  • There is no advertising and no analytics code in the app.
  • The version from Google Play makes no network connections. The APK from GitHub only asks GitHub whether a newer release exists, and sends no personal or cycle data.
  • Health Connect is read only and optional, and the readings stay on your phone.
  • The app is meant for people aged 13 and over.
